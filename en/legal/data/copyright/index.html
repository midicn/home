Data Copyright & Sources

No rights claimed over the works; per-track source licences; sources and removal logs

1 min readUpdated 2026-11-02

legalcopyrightdata

Inclusion does not transfer rights

Listing music or a SoundFont does not give this site every right in the composition, performance, recording, arrangement, transcription or samples. Licence information helps locate the source; it does not replace checking its original terms.

Checking an item

1. Record its file ID, title, source and licence marker from the detail page. 2. Open the original release and check the licence version, attribution and non-commercial or other use restrictions. 3. Confirm that permission covers the material and use you actually need. A public-domain composition does not make all recordings or editions unrestricted. 4. Keep the source and licence record used for the decision, so it can be checked during delivery or review.

See the data terms for C1, C2 and C3, and the content copyright page for site-owned explanations.

Disputed records

Report conflicting licences, attribution errors or disputed inclusion through the project issue page, with the file, original source and supporting evidence. The record will be checked and, where warranted, corrected or access adjusted.
