Music KnowledgeThe gateway to midicn's music knowledge sites — theory, instruments, history, composers, genres, production and Chinese music, each with its own site and all cross-linked.GlossaryA music glossary you can read as well as look things up in — terms, people, genres, instruments and Chinese music, each defined in a sentence and linked to deeper reading elsewhere.Music HistoryMusic history, period by period — from early music to the present, with the key milestones, representative works and listening cues of each era.TheoryA structured course in music theory and harmony — pitch, intervals, scales and modes, chords, harmonic progression, counterpoint, rhythm, melody, form, notation and performance practice. Every entry pairs playable examples with hand-drawn diagrams, all originally written in Chinese and English.InstrumentsAn illustrated atlas of instruments — Western, Chinese, electronic and world — with range charts, how each one makes its sound, and hand-drawn diagrams.Production & Digital AudioMusic production and digital audio — recording, mixing, synthesis and sound design, MIDI arranging and scoring for media, from first principles to practice.AI MusicMaking finished music with AI — tool choice, prompt to deliverable, controllability, hand-off and licensing.
