Where are you now

The same task needs different pages at different levels.

  1. 01Starting from zeroNo instrument, no notation. Build listening vocabulary first; do not buy gear yet.
  2. 02I play a littleA few pieces and simple notation. Connect technique to theory and repertoire.
  3. 03I write or arrangeYou finish songs, but mixing and delivery wobble. Fill in the production chain and standards.
  4. 04Professional or semi-proYou take paid work. Go straight to tool profiles, delivery specs and client workflow.
